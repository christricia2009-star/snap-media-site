How it works

Snap. Rank. Save. Trade.

Capture the title

Pick the format. Photograph the cover, spine, disc, J-card, or label. Or scan a barcode. Or add it by hand.

Review the shortlist

Assistive matches with confidence notes, year, and format. Open marketplace research if you want a second look.

Save to the shelf

Condition, paid, notes, photo. Wishlist if it isn’t yours yet. Export after you scan so a beta never eats the crate.

Trade if you want

Post extras, browse the feed, chat. Trades stay between collectors. The shelf stays local unless you sync or share.

Ownership

You own the shelf. That is a design constraint.

Independent collector app. Not a studio. Not a store. Not a grading service.

Photos

ID, then silence

A resized photo may go to the identification provider so the app can suggest a title. Photos are not sold. Manual add never sends a photo.

Estimates

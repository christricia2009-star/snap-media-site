FAQ

Straight answers for media collectors

What Snap Media does — and what it doesn’t claim.

Do I need Sign in with Apple? +

Sign in with Apple unlocks account features. Cloud backup and restore of your collection require you to be signed in. Local collecting works without an account; backup needs sign-in.

Why pick a category when I scan? +

Categories (DVD, VHS, Blu-ray, Cassette Tape, Records, Other) help focus identification. Choosing the right format often improves useful results — use Other for LaserDisc, MiniDisc, Betamax, and similar.

Is this official studio or label authentication? +

No. Snap Media offers AI-assisted identification for personal collecting and trading research. It is not official studio or label authentication, appraisal, or a guarantee of authenticity or value.

How do trades work? +

Trades are peer-to-peer between users. You can post offers, pick a title from inventory, browse the trade feed, open trade detail, and chat in-app. Set up contact info (username and Facebook profile for trust). Snap Media does not insure or guarantee trades.

Does cloud backup need sign-in? +

Yes. Backup and restore of your collection while signed in is available in the app. Stay signed in with Apple to use cloud backup.

What is the camera used for? +

The camera is for photographing media — covers, spines, discs, tapes, and sleeves — for identification and your collection. Photos support ID and cataloging, not unrelated uses.

How do I join the iOS beta? +

Snap Media for iOS is in beta. Email admin@snapcollectibles.com for beta access or support. There is no full public App Store launch claimed on this site until a live link is provided.

Is Android available? +

Android is coming soon. Reach out at admin@snapcollectibles.com if you want to be notified when Android testing opens.

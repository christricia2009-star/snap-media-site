Privacy / ownership

You own the shelf.

That is a design constraint, not a slogan. Snap Media is an independent collector app for iOS and Android. It is not a studio, not a store, and not a grading service.

What the app collects

The shelf — titles you add, notes, condition, purchase price, wishlist, for-sale flags, and photos you attach — lives on your device. Optional cloud backup is opt-in. You can export a full backup and import it later.

  • Sign-in is Apple on iOS, Google on Android, or continue as guest.
  • Trade username is optional and only needed if you post or accept trades.
  • Not directed at children under 13.

Photos and identification

When you Snap, Check, or scan a barcode, a resized photo or product lookup may be sent to the identification provider (OpenRouter) and optional market APIs so the app can suggest a title and comps. Photos are not sold. You can still add titles manually without sending a photo.

Estimates are estimates

Identifications and value ranges are estimates only — not professional appraisals, studio or label authentication, or guaranteed matches. Verify important titles yourself. Snap Media is not affiliated with any studio, label, or trademark owner. All trademarks belong to their respective owners.

Trades

Trades are peer-to-peer between collectors. Snap Media does not escrow items or money and does not insure trades. Contact details you put in the app are information you choose to share.

Subscriptions

Free includes a photo-ID allotment, your collection, and catalog browse. Optional Pro is an auto-renewing App Store or Google Play subscription. Manage or cancel it in the store account, not on this website.

What this site collects

This marketing site is static. The beta form sends App name, phone OS, and email to admin@snapcollectibles.com so we can issue a tester invite. No analytics SDK is bundled.

Android testers are shown the Play internal-test URL on the page after submit. Google Play does not automatically email testers. That URL stays inactive until the email is added to the tester list — allow up to a few hours.

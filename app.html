What the app does

On iPhone

Five tabs

  • Home
  • Collection
  • Snap
  • Trades
  • Settings

Snap a cover

The phone checks the on-device catalog first. If that misses, and only after you agree, the photo can be sent to identify the title.

Scan a UPC, or type it

A product lookup can name the barcode. A second lookup may run if the first misses.

Hand entry

Hand entry always works if you decline lookup.

Collection

Each copy can keep these fields:

  • Format
  • Shelf location
  • Price paid
  • Date bought
  • Store
  • Personal rating
  • Front and back photos
  • Extra photos
  • Box set with member titles
  • Watch dates
  • Loaned, for sale, or sold
  • TV seasons and episodes
  • Custom fields

Another format

The same movie in another format is a separate copy. The app warns when that title is already owned in a different format.

Sort and group

Sort by title, year, or date added. Group by format, shelf, genre, or decade.

Import

Import a CLZ Movies CSV export.

Stats

Count, runtime, amount spent, format, decade, and genre.

Trades, messages, and events

A trade username is enough to post. A Facebook link is optional.

Sign in and backup

Sign in with Apple. Face ID app lock. iCloud backup of the private collection. Export a backup file.

Report or block

Report a trade or event, and block a user. Abusive language is blocked before it is posted.

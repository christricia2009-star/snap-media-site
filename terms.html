Terms of Use

Last updated October 4, 2026

Snap Media is a personal catalog for physical media you own. These terms are the end-user license for the app.

Plus is an auto-renewable subscription: $1.99 a month or $14.99 a year. Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation. The subscription renews unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Apple charges the renewal within 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

Titles you add or edit while Plus is active stay in your collection if you cancel. Those titles become view-only so they cannot be renamed into different movies. You can still view them, export them, and delete your entire account and collection without an active subscription.

Trades, messages, and events are user content. There is no tolerance for illegal, hateful, or abusive posts, or for anything that infringes someone else’s rights. The app blocks that language before it is posted. You can report a trade or event and block a user. We remove that content and may restrict the account that posted it.
